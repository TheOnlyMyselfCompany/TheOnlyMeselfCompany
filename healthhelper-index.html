The one everyone should know

💧 ORS — the rehydration drink

For loose motions or vomiting, losing water is the real danger. This simple drink is the fix, and it has saved millions of lives.

1 litreclean water
6 level tspsugar
½ level tspsalt
Level means flat, not heaped — scrape the spoon with a knife. Give small sips often, and a glass after every loose motion.
Careful: never add extra salt — too much salt is dangerous, especially for small children. Use plain white sugar (not honey or jaggery), and throw away anything left after 24 hours. A shop-bought ORS packet is even safer if you have one.

If your head hurts more than your body

💚 Feeling low is health too

If you're feeling really down, very anxious, or having thoughts of hurting yourself — that deserves help just like a fever does, and it's not something to push through alone. Tell someone you trust: a parent, a teacher, an older sibling.

In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 — free, 24×7, in many languages. If someone is in immediate danger, call 112.
